AI can be used to create art, and make music. But sometimes, people will take it and copy others using AI or make thier own and say they made it without AI. This is not good because people could listen to the music and they wont know if its AI, and the person can make money off of something that isnt originally theirs.

One article states that "Music fans are becoming increasingly uncomfortable with AI songs, according to a recent report published by the music and entertainment insights company Luminate. The decline is especially notable with young listeners who are part of Gen Z and Gen Alpha." I agree with this because kids and teens could grow up listening to songs made by an AI bot and the human is making money of its work.
